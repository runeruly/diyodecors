Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions weddings customers ask us most.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 2-3 months ahead for weddings, especially for peak-season dates. Reach out early to reserve your date.

Do you offer mandap and stage packages?

Yes. We design and build full mandaps, reception stages, and lounge setups tailored to your venue and theme.

Can you match a cultural or destination theme?

Absolutely. We blend traditional elements — brass, marigolds, red & gold drapes — with modern luxury for Nepali, Indian, and fusion weddings.

Do you work with our venue or vendors?

We collaborate with your venue and coordinate with planners, florists, and caterers to ensure a seamless experience.
