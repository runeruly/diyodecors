Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions special pujas customers ask us most.

Which pujas do you decorate for?

We style setup for Griha Pravesh, Satyanarayan, Navratri, birthdays, anniversaries, and other special pujas.

Do you bring the sacred items?

We provide décor; ritual items like kalash, thali, and offerings are typically arranged by the family.

Yes, we create beautiful setups in both intimate homes and larger halls.
