Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions bartabanda customers ask us most.

What makes a Bartabanda setup authentic?

We use traditional brass vessels, fresh marigold garlands, red-and-gold fabrics, and ritual seating to honor the ceremony.

Can you decorate a mandir or hall?

Yes, we style homes, mandirs, community halls, and banquet venues.

Do you coordinate with the priest?

We arrange the décor around the ritual flow and can coordinate timing with the priest and family.

How far in advance should we book?

We suggest booking 4-6 weeks ahead, especially during festival and auspicious-date seasons.
